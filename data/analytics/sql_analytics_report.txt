Query 01: Monthly Sales & Revenue Growth
  - Business Question: How has sales volume, product revenue, freight, and MoM GMV evolved over time?
  - Key Finding: Analyzed 23 operational months (2016-09 to 2018-08). Peak monthly GMV reached R$ 1.15M
    in November 2017 (Black Friday campaign), representing +47.5% MoM expansion over October 2017.

Query 02: Product Category Performance
  - Business Question: Which product categories drive the highest merchandise revenue and units sold?
  - Key Finding: Analyzed 74 distinct categories. Top category is 'health_beauty' with R$ 1.44M GMV (9,670 units),
    followed by 'watches_gifts' (R$ 1.31M) and 'bed_bath_table' (R$ 1.25M across 11,115 units sold).
    Total category revenue reconciles exactly to R$ 15,843,553.24 (0.00 difference with warehouse fact).

Query 03: Delivery Performance & Logistics SLA
  - Business Question: What is shipping speed, SLA delay rate, and review rating across Brazilian destination states?
  - Key Finding: Nationwide average delivery is 12.1 days. Sao Paulo (SP) boasts fastest transit (8.3 days,
    5.5% late delivery rate, 4.25 avg rating). Northern states (RR, AP, AM) experience 26-29 day transits
    with late delivery rates reaching 16-20%, directly correlating with lower customer satisfaction scores.

Query 04: Customer RFM Segmentation
  - Business Question: How do customers segment across Recency, Frequency, and Monetary dimensions?
  - Key Finding: Segmented all 93,358 unique consumers. 8,128 'Champions' average R$ 430.79 basket spend and account
    for R$ 3.50M in sales. 14,768 valuable past buyers are identified as 'At Risk' (R$ 4.52M historical spend),
    providing immediate targeting candidates for automated re-engagement email campaigns.

Query 05: Payment Value Tiers & Ticket Distribution
  - Business Question: How is order value distributed across basket sizes, and what is the checkout concentration?
  - Key Finding: Mid-ticket orders (R$ 100 - R$ 250) form the revenue backbone, generating 37.55% of all marketplace
    payments. Orders over R$ 500 generate 24.76% of total payments despite representing only 8.44% of total order volume.

Query 06: Geographic Sales Performance
  - Business Question: Which Brazilian states dominate purchasing power and what is their freight burden ratio?
  - Key Finding: State of Sao Paulo (SP) represents 37.44% of total national marketplace revenue (R$ 5.77M GMV,
    40,494 delivered orders). Top 5 states (SP, RJ, MG, RS, PR) generate over 78% of all national e-commerce volume.

Query 07: Merchant Fulfillment Performance (Pareto Analysis)
  - Business Question: How concentrated is merchant revenue, and does an 80/20 Pareto distribution apply?
  - Key Finding: Evaluated all 3,095 sellers. Top 10 merchants generate R$ 2.03M (12.8% of marketplace sales).
    The top 20% of active merchants (~619 sellers) generate approximately 83.4% of total cumulative marketplace sales,
    validating a classic Pareto distribution in e-commerce marketplace supply dynamics.

4. ARCHITECTURAL DECISIONS & WAREHOUSE LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Query 05 Architectural Note:
    In Phase 4, financial measures were modeled cleanly at the order grain (dwh.fact_order.total_payment)
    and order item grain (dwh.fact_order_item.price). Individual payment methods (credit_card, voucher, boleto)
    exhibit a 1-to-many relationship with orders (103,886 payment transactions across 99,440 orders).
    Rather than compromising warehouse integrity by querying unverified raw staging tables or forcing
    non-conforming granularities into fact_order, Query 05 was designed to evaluate order-level payment tier
    distribution supported natively by the warehouse. A dedicated dwh.fact_order_payment table can be
    added in future iterations if transaction-level tender analysis is required.
